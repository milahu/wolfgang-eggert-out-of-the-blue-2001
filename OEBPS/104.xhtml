Politische Bedeutung/Rußland
18 September 2000. Russian President Vladimir Putin praised
 the Russian Jewish community's revival at a ceremony
 dedicating a Lubavitch-sponsored Jewish Community Center,
 the first of its kind in the region. During a speech Monday
 seen as an unprecedented demonstration of Kremlin support
 for Russia's Jewish community, Putin also praised the
 Lubavitch-dominated Federation of Jewish Communities as a
 "highly constructive and influential organization." One of
 Israel's former chief rabbis, Mordechai Eliyahu, presented
 Putin with a shofar.
